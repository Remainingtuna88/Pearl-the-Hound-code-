Edit diagram data

Everything on the canvas comes from this data. Change it here and press Apply; it’s saved in this browser. To make a change permanent for everyone, press Copy JSON and paste it over the DIAGRAM block at the top of the page’s script.
  • A box: id, title, file, lane, summary, and optional kind ("prompt" or "external").
  • rows: ["key", "name", "type", "what it does"]. Keys: C class, F function, T thread, K setting, S step, E endpoint, R prompt line.
  • links: ["target id", "label"], or add "dashed" for a network call.
  • Prompt boxes take the full prompt in text; leave out rows and each line becomes a row.
  • Leave out x and y and the box stacks itself in its lane.
